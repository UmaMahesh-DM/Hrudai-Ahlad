How We Make It

The Ancient Art of Cold Pressing

01
Source the Best
We handpick seeds and grains directly from trusted organic farms — groundnuts, sesame, coconuts — no middlemen, maximum freshness.
02
Sun Dry & Clean
Natural sun drying removes moisture without chemicals. Each batch is carefully cleaned and sorted by hand before pressing.
03
Wood Press (Ganuga)
The traditional wooden Ganuga press spins slowly — never heating above 40°C. This preserves every enzyme, vitamin and omega fatty acid.
04
Natural Settle & Filter
Oils naturally settle and filter over 24 hours. No centrifuges, no bleaching, no deodorising — just pure, raw, golden oil.
05
Packed Fresh to You
Bottled in food-grade glass or BPA-free containers. Delivered within days of pressing — freshness you can smell and taste.
